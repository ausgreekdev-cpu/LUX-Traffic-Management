Privacy Policy — Delux TPM CRM / LUX Field

Effective: 7 Sep 2026 • AusGreek Developments • Contact: ausgreekdev@gmail.com

Delux TPM CRM and LUX Field (“we”, “us”) respect your privacy. This policy applies to the web app at lux-official.netlify.app and the Android field app com.lux.traffic.field on Google Play.

Data We Collect

  • Account: name, email, role, client linkage, tenant ID. Passwords bcrypt-hashed.
  • Project: TMPs, sites, permits, time entries, fees, documents.
  • Camera / Photos: site photos you capture via the field app (with optional geotag). Stored via /api/photos.
  • Location: optional GPS coordinates attached to site photos (foreground only, with consent). No background tracking.
  • Telemetry: anonymized usage counters for billing limits.

Permissions (Android)

  • CAMERA — capture site photos
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — geotag photos (foreground only)
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES — pick existing photos
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — sync to https://lux-official.netlify.app

No advertising, no sale of data, no third-party analytics. No tracking across apps.

Storage & Retention

Web/Netlify: SQLite in Netlify Blobs, media via media-store. Desktop: local SQLite. Photos retained until deleted by Manager/Developer. Backups encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM).

Your Rights

Request access, correction, or deletion via ausgreekdev@gmail.com. Tenant admins can delete users/assets in Settings → Security.

Data Safety (Google Play)

We declare: Email, Name, Photos, Location (optional, foreground). Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. No data shared with third parties.

Children
